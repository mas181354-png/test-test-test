# Frame Generation Prompts

Every storyboard frame was generated with **Google Nano Banana 2** (`imagen-nano-banana-2-flash`) via Magnific, at **16:9, 2K**.

**Consistency method:** Shot 2 was generated first, then passed back as an image reference for shots 3, 5 and 8 so the actress's face, hair and wardrobe stay consistent across the lifestyle beats. If you re-roll shot 2, re-roll 3, 5 and 8 against the new version.

**Shared style spine** (present in every lifestyle prompt): *cinematic commercial film still, warm golden-hour interior light, shallow depth of field, modern Scandinavian living room, high-end lifestyle TV commercial grade, photorealistic, no text overlay, no watermark, no brand logos.*

The `no text overlay` instruction is deliberate — all typography is added in the edit, not baked into the frame.

## Re-rolling tips

- **Button layout wrong?** Generative models will not reproduce the exact BN59-01315J key arrangement or the printed logo text. Either accept it for pre-vis and shoot the product for real, or upload a photo of your actual unit as a `product` reference to pull the geometry closer.
- **Actress drifted between shots?** Regenerate the offending frame with shot 2 attached as an image reference and keep the wardrobe description verbatim.
- **Need vertical versions?** Re-generate at 9:16 rather than cropping — a centre-crop of the 16:9 wide shot loses the room distance that shot 5 depends on.
- **Want a different lead?** Change the description in shot 2 only, then re-reference shots 3, 5 and 8 from the new anchor.
